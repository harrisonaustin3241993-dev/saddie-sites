Leftwise Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Leftwise is made by Harrison App Lab LLC ("we"). The short version: we don't collect your data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code in the app.

Your recipes and plans

Recipes, week plans, grocery lists and settings are stored on your iPhone only. They are not uploaded to us or to any server. To keep a backup, use Export in Settings.

Importing recipes

From a web link: when you import a link, your iPhone downloads that page directly from the recipe site, the same way a browser would. The site sees an ordinary request from your device. The link is not sent to us.

From screenshots, photos or pasted text: the text is read and organized on your iPhone using Apple's on-device text recognition and, where available, Apple's on-device language model. Your images and text are not uploaded to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Leftwise Standard and Plus are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never receive your name, card or billing details; the app only learns whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information, usage data, location or contacts.
  • We don't show ads or share or sell data.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you email us

If you write to support, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply. We delete support emails once the issue is resolved, and at the latest after 12 months.

Children

Leftwise is a meal-planning tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app does with data (for example, optional sync), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released, and the date above will change.
