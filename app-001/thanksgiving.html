THANKSGIVING WEEK

Turkey on Thursday. Dinner through Sunday.

Leftwise plans your week's dinners from recipes you save, and builds one grocery list for only what's missing.

Get Leftwise on the App Store

iPhone. Saving recipes is free. Standard $0.99/mo and Plus $3.99/mo, each with a free trial.

How it works with leftovers

  1. Save a few recipes that use what's in your fridge: turkey soup, pot pie, tacos. Paste a link or snap a screenshot.
  2. Pick your dinners for the week. Leftwise shows which ingredients the meals share.
  3. Get one grocery list with duplicates merged, so you only buy what you don't already have.
