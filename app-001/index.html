Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Leftwise works without signing in, and your recipes are kept on your iPhone.

How do I move my recipes to a new phone? In Leftwise, open Settings and use Export all as JSON, then save or send the file to yourself. Sync across devices is coming in a future update.

A recipe link didn't import. Leftwise reads recipes from sites that publish standard recipe data. If a site doesn't, take a screenshot of the recipe or copy the text, and import that instead.

How do I cancel Leftwise Standard or Plus? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Leftwise, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. To avoid being charged after a free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Your saved recipes and plans stay in the app whatever your plan.

How do I restore a purchase? In Leftwise, open Settings > See plans and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.
