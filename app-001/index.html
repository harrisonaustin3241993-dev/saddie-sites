Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Leftwise works without signing in. Your recipes are kept on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud storage.

My recipes aren't showing on my new phone. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Leftwise in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes after first launch.

A recipe link didn't import. Leftwise reads recipes from sites that publish standard recipe data. If a site doesn't, take a screenshot of the recipe or copy the text, and import that instead.

How do I cancel Leftwise Plus? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Leftwise, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I restore a purchase? In Leftwise, open Settings > Leftwise Plus and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I delete my data? Deleting the app removes the data on your phone. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage, choose Leftwise, and delete its data.
